### G13 · Possession with consonant final nouns

Earliest explicit table in this sequence. Consonant-final possessives need a linking vowel in many forms. Third-person j is lexical: learn each noun. The source lists tendencies, not universal rules: many sibilant/palatal-final nouns omit j, but bátyja is an exception. Back-vowel stems more often have j; v/l/r/m/g/k-final stems more often omit it; b/d-final stems often take it.
