## Grammar and progression

### G27 · Megy jön and jár

Revisited or practiced; teaching order may be uncertain. Megy indicates going away/toward a destination; jön indicates coming toward the speaker, accompanying the speaker, or approaching a contextually adopted location. The choice can depend on viewpoint. Jár is contrasted with megy as regular/habitual travel.

| Person     | megy    | jön    |
| ---------- | ------- | ------ |
| én         | megyek  | jövök  |
| te         | mész    | jössz  |

| mi         | megyünk | jövünk |
| ti         | mentek  | jöttök |

## Grammar and progression

### G28 · Instrumental accompaniment and means

Earliest explicit table in this sequence. Kivel? asks with whom and Mivel? asks with what/by what means. Add -val/-vel after vowels, with final a/e lengthening. After a consonant, v assimilates to that consonant; already long consonants are not tripled. Possessive suffixes precede the instrumental suffix.

autóval, biciklivel, repülővel; vonattal, busszal, Péterrel, Jánossal, Mihállyal, tollal; a barátommal, a tanárukkal.

### G28 · Instrumental accompaniment and means

Kivel? asks with whom and Mivel? asks with what/by what means. Add -val/-vel after vowels, with final a/e lengthening. After a consonant, v assimilates to that consonant; already long consonants are not tripled. Possessive suffixes precede the instrumental suffix.

autóval, biciklivel, repülővel; vonattal, busszal, Péterrel, Jánossal, Mihállyal, tollal; a barátommal, a tanárukkal.
