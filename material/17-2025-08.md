## Grammar and progression

### G10 · Questions and focus

Revisited or practiced; teaching order may be uncertain. Yes/no questions are taught through intonation and punctuation, without obligatory word-order inversion. Question words: ki person, mi thing/action, hol location, hány count, melyik choice, milyen quality/type, miért reason. In the practiced question-word pattern the focused question phrase immediately precedes the conjugated verb.

Beszélsz magyarul? Hol él Erika? Milyen nyelven tanul Géza? Miért tanulsz franciául? Mert ... . These are teaching patterns, not a complete account of every Hungarian word order.

### G15 · Possessive and case suffix order

Earliest explicit table in this sequence. When a noun takes several endings, the taught order is noun + possessive suffix + case suffix. A possessive stem may change before a later suffix.

a városom → a városomban; a könyved → a könyvedben; a lakásunk → a lakásunkban; az irodája → az irodájában; a táskámban.
