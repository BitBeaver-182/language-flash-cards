### G03 · Articles a az and egy

Revisited or practiced; teaching order may be uncertain. Use a before a consonant and az before a vowel. Egy introduces new information or a quantity. A/az can refer back to something known and normally precedes possessed nouns. Profession predicates are taught without an article.
