### G18 · Infinitive tud lehet and other governing verbs

Revisited or practiced; teaching order may be uncertain. The basic infinitive suffix is -ni; some learned stems use -ani/-eni. Tud conjugates for personal ability: tudok, tudsz, tud, tudunk, tudtok, tudnak. Lehet + infinitive expresses possibility/permission in the teaching sheet. Infinitives also follow learned verbs such as segít, tanul, kezd and próbál.

### G18 · Infinitive tud lehet and other governing verbs

Revisited or practiced; teaching order may be uncertain. The basic infinitive suffix is -ni; some learned stems use -ani/-eni. Tud conjugates for personal ability: tudok, tudsz, tud, tudunk, tudtok, tudnak. Lehet + infinitive expresses possibility/permission in the teaching sheet. Infinitives also follow learned verbs such as segít, tanul, kezd and próbál.

### G19 · Demonstratives and preferences

Revisited or practiced; teaching order may be uncertain. Near the speaker: ez; farther away: az. Before a noun, retain the definite article: ez a/ez az and az a/az az. Tetszik expresses liking in the practiced object-focused questions. Reasons use mert; opinions use szerintem. Sem expresses also not/neither in the source dialogues.

### G17 · Negation nem nincs and hanem

Whole-clause verbal negation places nem before the verb. Third-person existential/locative van becomes nincs/nincsen and vannak becomes nincsenek. Identity/adjective predicates use nem without inserting van. Contrastive negation places nem before the negated phrase and may continue with hanem.

Nem beszélek magyarul. Nem vagyok otthon. A tanár nincs a szobában. A diákok nincsenek az iskolában. Nem Péter tanul Rómában, hanem Géza. Nem otthon van, hanem az irodában.

**Reviewed labelled vocabulary:**

hanem = but rather; nem = not; no; nincs = there is not; is not present; nincsen = there is not; is not present (variant).
