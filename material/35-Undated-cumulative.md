### G10 · Questions and focus

Yes/no questions are taught through intonation and punctuation, without obligatory word-order inversion. Question words: ki person, mi thing/action, hol location, hány count, melyik choice, milyen quality/type, miért reason. In the practiced question-word pattern the focused question phrase immediately precedes the conjugated verb.

Beszélsz magyarul? Hol él Erika? Milyen nyelven tanul Géza? Miért tanulsz franciául? Mert ... . These are teaching patterns, not a complete account of every Hungarian word order.

### G13 · Possession with consonant final nouns
